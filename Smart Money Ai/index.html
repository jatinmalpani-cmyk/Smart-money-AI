Quick Guidance

Use quality factors to narrow your allocation with value discipline.

• ROE above 20% is a strong signal that management is delivering profitable returns on equity.

• Debt/EBITDA below 3.0 typically indicates a manageable leverage profile.

• Operating margin above 20% often points to durable competitive advantages.
